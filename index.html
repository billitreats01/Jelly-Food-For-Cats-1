How Can You Build a Consistent Feeding Routine?

A consistent feeding routine helps you monitor your cat's appetite, recognise changes in eating habits and ensure it receives regular nourishment. When caring for a growing kitten, combining age-appropriate meals with suitable options like jelly food for cats can add variety to daily feeding. Follow these simple habits to support healthy eating and attentive kitten care:

  • Set regular meal times: Choose times that suit your household and your cat’s age.
  • Measure portions: Follow feeding guidance and consider weight, activity and life stage.
  • Serve fresh food: Store opened packs as directed and discard leftovers safely.
  • Choose a quiet spot: Keep bowls away from litter trays and busy walkways.
  • Track intake: Notice whether your cat finishes meals or suddenly eats less.

Appetite can vary, but repeated changes deserve attention. If your cat refuses food, loses weight or seems unwell, contact a veterinarian instead of relying on a new flavour.
